## Objective: 
implement a contact syste.


## route- /contact-requests
 the ideia is to provide a route /contact-requests 
that recives a json body with a limited size (configured on the backoffice) , these jsons can be of any type, (array, objects ,etc), in these way the front end can chose formats.


## backoffice-fomrs-visualization 
in the backoffice area , the backoffice users must be able to visualize the concat jsons, and the time that that json was created, and the ip and country of contactor. 

## managment 
the root can archive or delete contacts , and search by date , country ,or "contains" (the contact json)
