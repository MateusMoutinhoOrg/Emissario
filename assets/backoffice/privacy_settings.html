IPs

Views, contact requests and agent usage. Hashed: an HMAC under a random key kept here — it counts visitors and matches an abuser, and cannot be reversed without the key. It is still personal data (pseudonymous). Full asks for a notice. A change applies to new rows.
Hashed or full, an IP older than this is blanked every hour; its record stays until its own retention ends. 184 by default; past 365, or blank, asks for a notice.

Access log (Marco Civil)

Off by default. Turning it on asks for a notice. Turning it off stops new rows; the rows kept stay until their retention ends.

Danger zone

Hash the IPs already stored

Every full IP of the records becomes its hash under the current IP key. The access log is kept as it is.

Rotate IP key

A new random key: the hashes kept can no longer be matched to new visits nor confirmed against an IP — the way to forget the linkage early, after a server compromise for one.
