# Access log

The access log keeps every visitor's full IP, source port and time. A dynamic IP is personal data (CJEU C-582/14 *Breyer*; LGPD art. 5 I).

- **Brazil:** a company running this website for profit **must** keep it 6 months (Marco Civil art. 15), and that **legal obligation** is its legal basis (LGPD art. 7 II). Keep it confidential, disclose it only on a court order (art. 10), and use it for nothing else: statistics and blocking use the records (LGPD art. 6 I–III). An authority may ask you to keep rows longer (art. 15 §2): put them on hold, and keep the request confidential (art. 13 §4).
- **EU:** there is no general duty to keep IPs. Turn it on only for a security need, under a legitimate interest with a documented balancing test (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)), within the CNIL's 6 months to 1 year.

By default Emissario applies Decreto 8.771/2016 art. 13 for you: named readers, two-factor sign-in, an inventory of reads, encryption. During its term a row is kept even when its person asks for erasure (LGPD art. 16 I; GDPR art. 17(3)(b)).
