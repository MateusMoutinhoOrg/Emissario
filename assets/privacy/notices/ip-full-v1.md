# Full IPs

Full IPs identify visitors. A dynamic IP is personal data (CJEU C-582/14 *Breyer*; LGPD art. 5 I). Keeping them in views, contact requests and agent usage, with the full user agent of each view, needs a legal basis, usually a legitimate interest in security, fraud prevention or statistics with a documented balancing test (LGPD art. 10; GDPR art. 6(1)(f)), the shortest retention that serves it (LGPD art. 6 III; GDPR art. 5(1)(c)), and a line in your privacy notice.

- A full IP with its time is also an access log (Marco Civil art. 5 VIII): keep it confidential and disclose it only on a court order (art. 10).
- It does not replace the access log of Marco Civil art. 15: it has no source port and goes with its record. A for-profit company in Brazil still turns the access log on.
- Viewers see these IPs masked unless you choose otherwise.
