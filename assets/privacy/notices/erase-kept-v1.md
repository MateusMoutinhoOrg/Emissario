# Deleting access log rows inside their term

You are deleting access log rows inside their term. A company that runs this website for profit in Brazil must keep them 6 months (Marco Civil art. 15), and the laws do not require erasing them on a person's request (LGPD art. 16 I; GDPR art. 17(3)(b)). Delete them when that duty does not apply to you, such as an EU or non-profit website.
