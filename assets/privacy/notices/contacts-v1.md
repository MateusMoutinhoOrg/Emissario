# Contact requests

Turning on contact requests makes this server store what visitors send through your form (names, emails, phones, messages) with its time and the visitor's IP, hashed unless you chose `full`. **You are the controller of this data.** That is lawful as long as you:

- **tell visitors** in your privacy notice what is kept, why, for how long and how to ask for its deletion (LGPD art. 9; GDPR art. 13);
- have a **legal basis**: steps the visitor asked for before a contract (LGPD art. 7 V; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)), or your legitimate interest in answering other messages (LGPD art. 7 IX; GDPR art. 6(1)(f)), and do not use it for marketing without consent;
- watch for **sensitive data** (health, religion, biometrics…) and **minors**: a form that may receive them needs the stricter rules of LGPD arts. 11 and 14 and GDPR arts. 8 and 9, and a website likely used by minors falls under the ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025);
- keep it **only while needed**: Emissario deletes requests older than the retention you set (LGPD arts. 15–16; GDPR art. 5(1)(e));
- **answer** access and deletion requests (LGPD arts. 18–19, a full answer within 15 days; GDPR arts. 12(3) and 15–17, within one month);
- **record every incident** for 5 years, and **report** one that may bring relevant risk to the ANPD and to the people affected within 3 business days (Res. CD/ANPD 15/2024), and to the EU authority within 72 h (GDPR art. 33);
- name an **encarregado** and publish their identity and contact (LGPD art. 41; Res. CD/ANPD 18/2024; only small-scale agents are spared, Res. CD/ANPD 2/2022), and appoint an **EU representative** if you are outside the EU and target people there (GDPR art. 27).

Fines: LGPD up to 2% of revenue in Brazil, capped at R$ 50 million per infraction (art. 52). GDPR up to €20 million or 4% of worldwide turnover (art. 83).
