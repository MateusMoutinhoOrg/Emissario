# The agent

Every message a visitor sends goes to the model provider you configured (`<host>`). That provider processes it on your behalf: sign its data processing terms (GDPR art. 28), under which it does **not use the conversations to train its models** or for its own purposes. Otherwise it is not your processor but another controller, and you are sharing the data with it. A provider in another country is an international transfer. Between Brazil and the EU, adequacy covers it (Res. CD/ANPD 32/2026; Commission decision of 26 Jan 2026). Elsewhere, use the standard clauses of Res. CD/ANPD 19/2024 (LGPD art. 33) and GDPR arts. 44–49 (the EU–US Data Privacy Framework covers certified US providers only). Visitors may type **sensitive data**, like a clinic's patients writing about their health: your notice and your legal basis must cover it (LGPD art. 11; GDPR art. 9). A local model (Ollama) keeps conversations on your infrastructure. The agent says it is an AI in its first answer, and **your chat UI must also tell visitors they are talking to an AI** (EU AI Act art. 50(1), applicable since 2 August 2026).
